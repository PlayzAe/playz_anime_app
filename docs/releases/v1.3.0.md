This release has two downloads: the Windows app (1.3.0) and, for the first time, the Android app (2.2.0).

| You're on | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 / 11 | `PlayzAnime-Setup-1.3.0.exe` (installer) or `PlayzAnime-Portable-1.3.0.exe` |

## Android 2.2.0 (new)

PlayzAnime is now on Android: I rebuilt the desktop app for touch. Right now it's my most up-to-date version, but it still has issues, and I'm looking for people to help me. Found a bug? Please open an issue or a pull request.

* **Player:**
  * Opens in fullscreen landscape by itself; the exit button returns to the episode list.
  * Double-tap to skip 5 seconds, swipe for brightness and volume, hold for 2×, lock the screen, picture-in-picture.
  * Quality picker, subtitle style (size, colour, background, outline, position), sub or dub, and a source-player fallback with a way back.
  * Play, pause and next in the notification shade.
* **Downloads:**
  * Pick an episode range, quality (with sizes), audio and subtitles before downloading. Hold an episode to select several.
  * Progress in your notifications. Downloads keep going with the screen off, renew expired stream links, wait out lost connections and continue after a restart.
  * A Downloads page grouped by show, filtered by Anime, Manga, Manhwa and Manhua.
  * With no internet, the app opens on your downloads.
* **Reader:** Paged left to right, right to left or vertical, long strip, long strip with gaps, an adjustable page width, and fit width or fit screen.
* **Setup and profile:**
  * The first launch asks for permissions properly and lets you choose your anime and manga folders.
  * Your accent colour changes the app icon on your home screen.
  * The profile page sets your name and picture, and exports or imports your list and settings.
* **Extensions (optional):**
  * Manga works without them. For extra sites I took inspiration from how Mihon handles extensions, so the app can load the ones from the [keiyoushi repo](https://github.com/keiyoushi/extensions).
  * They update once a day, only load when you use them, and NSFW-rated ones hide while "Hide adult content" is on.
  * Still buggy: Flutter and Dart can't run them directly, so some sources fail or need a retry.

Install: open the APK on your phone and allow your browser to install unknown apps. If Play Protect warns, tap **More details → Install anyway**. [Full install guide](https://playzae.github.io/playz_anime_landingpage/docs/android/install-on-android)

## Windows 1.3.0

* **A New Downloads Page:**
  * I rebuilt the Downloads page so it works like a home page instead of one long list.
  * Downloads are split into tabs: Anime, Manga, Manhwa and Manhua.
  * Everything in progress is grouped into one card per series, showing how much is done, waiting and paused. 3,000 chapters of one series are now one card, not 3,000 rows.
  * Finished downloads show as a shelf of covers. Click one to play or read, jump to any episode or chapter, or find a chapter by number.
* **Pause, Resume and Cancel:**
  * I added pause and resume for a single download, a whole series, or everything at once.
  * Cancel now actually cancels: it stops the download and deletes what it had saved.
  * I added Cancel all, which stops and deletes every unfinished download. Finished downloads stay.
  * You can now choose how many downloads run at once (1 to 8), on the Downloads page or in Settings.
  * Downloads left unfinished when you close the app now come back paused instead of as errors. Press Resume all to carry on.
* **Clearer Download Choices:**
  * Chapters: download from the chapter you're on, unread chapters, a range (chapter X to Y, with Next 10, 50 and 100 shortcuts), or all of them. The count shows before you start.
  * Episodes: download only this episode, a range from one episode to another, or all of them.
  * The download button on a chapter row now downloads just that chapter.
* **Bug Fixes:**
  * Fixed the download list only keeping the newest 300 downloads. Big batches were pushing finished downloads out of the list and losing queued chapters on restart. Nothing gets dropped now.
  * I added "Find downloads in my folders", which brings files already on your PC back into the list with their covers. It runs by itself once if the list has no finished downloads.
  * Fixed the app slowing down with thousands of downloads queued.
  * Fixed the Extensions list in Settings showing "0 of 0".
  * The taskbar icon now follows the accent colour you pick.
* **Also Included:**
  * The file uploaded to the v1.2.0 release was an older 1.1.0 build. This release includes all the v1.2.0 improvements: a smaller download (about 90 MB, down from 99 MB) and the redesigned Settings page.
* **Testing:**
  * Ran the offline self-test before and after the changes, and it passed both times.
  * Tested the new Downloads page on a copy of a real profile with more than 3,000 queued chapters.
